Finding · structural

σ′n and τ are the same number

new affects the headline metric, not any run

Every deck computes both stress channels from one postprocessor. Read straight out of 93_07:

σ′n = 30.0 − 0.5·(Pi + Po) + sin²θ · σd τ = sinθcosθ · σd

Pi and Po are the prescribed schedule and the model reproduces them to better than 0.1 MPa. So both channels are affine images of a single model output, differential_stress_reaction_mpa_pp, and their errors are locked at τerr = cotθ · σn,err.

That is exactly what the residuals do. Across five runs and 54 stages the two error columns fall on one line through the origin, with the slope recovering each deck's own fracture dip.

The redundancy is on the target side too. Fitting θ and Po to Table 2's own σ′n and τ columns recovers each deck's declared dip and a clean 5 MPa outlet pressure, with residuals at the rounding of the published table:

Two consequences. The scorecard has four independent observables, not five — Q, the frame's differential stress, dn, ds. And because τ has a narrower paper range than σ′n, the same physical error is amplified about 2.6× more in the τ column, so the mean quietly weights one quantity far above the rest: 25% of SW-T1's reported error, 28% of SW-T2's, and 46–48% of the two shear specimens'.

Nothing here invalidates a run. It does mean that a chunk of what looked like independent corroboration was one number counted twice, and the deck's fracture dips are now independently validated against the paper.

Finding · SW-T1

The bracket closed, and it closed against a cliff

reverses an earlier call

The Vm search had never turned around, which is why 105_01–03 pushed to 70, 90 and 110 µm. It turns between 55 and 70, and past roughly 80 µm the model stops reproducing the experiment at all.

At 90 µm the fracture slips 0.0156 mm against 0.537 mm in the working runs, the hydraulic aperture never leaves its initial 1.63 µm, and τ ends the run at 65.96 MPa instead of 27.87. The specimen simply locks.

The reason it locks is worth recording on its own. At stage 5 the working run carries 67.73 MPa of shear against a 69.04 MPa strength limit — a margin of 1.31 MPa, 1.9% of the limit. Raising Vm softens the joint, the axial ram spends more of its stroke closing it, less load reaches the fault, and the margin widens just enough that the event never triggers. The whole experiment turns on a switch with a 2% throw, which is why single-parameter arms in this campaign either barely move or fall off a cliff.

Finding · SW-S4

One stage, and both arms bracketed it from the wrong side

reverses an earlier call

SW-S4's residual is not spread across the run. Stage 4 carries τ +2.74 MPa with ds at 0.0034 mm against 0.017 measured — the experiment has started slipping and the model has not. Every other stage is between +0.05 and +1.26.

Shortening Dc did fix onset — ds at stage 4 goes to 0.018 mm against 0.017 measured — but it then spends the entire weakening curve inside one stage. Dc sets how fast weakening runs once started; it cannot make it start earlier without also making it finish sooner.

Reading the implied Dc back out of Table 2's own τ–ds pairs gives 55–98 µm. The parent's 74.5 µm was already right. So was the residual floor: the parent's unloading τ error is +0.89 falling to +0.05, and 105_05 pushed it through zero to −1.12. The untested direction is the diagonal — lower peak strength so onset lands at stage 4, with Dc held at 74.5. All three 105 arms moved along the post-onset axis instead.

Finding · hydraulics

The tensile specimens have no aperture law

new Q is the largest remaining channel on both tensile specimens

In SW-T1's best run, normal_stress_aperture_pp is identically zero at every timestep. The deck sets normal_stress_aperture_compliance = 0.0 and use_nonlinear_normal_closure = false, and the source gates the stress term on exactly those two. The hydraulic aperture sits frozen at its initial 1.630 µm through stages 1–5 while the measured aperture opens to 1.72.

SW-T2 has the nonlinear branch switched on, which is worse in a more interesting way. Its scale stress is

σ₀ = Vh · Kh = 1.2 µm × 1.25e13 Pa/m = 15.0 MPa

against an operating range of 58–67 MPa. Four times past its scale stress the law is saturated: it delivers 0.0023 µm of opening where Table 2 shows 0.58. At stage 5 the model produces 0.730 ml/min against 1.505 measured, off by 51.5%.

This is the same pathology the Vm bracket found in the mechanical closure law, in the hydraulic one, never refitted. Both tensile decks carry the same two constants verbatim. And it is fixable without a sweep — pre-event there is no dilation and no slip damage, so the aperture law is the only thing moving and it can be fitted directly to Table 2's own column:

The refitted hydraulic law describes the same joint as the mechanical one, which is what it should have been doing all along; the deck's 1.25e13 is fifty times stiffer than the joint it sits on.

Finding · provenance

Four specimens, one set of joint constants

new

All four decks carry an identical mechanical closure quadruple — Kni 2.443e11, Vm 45.91 µm, p 3.28, offset 44.33 µm — across specimens whose JRC spans 1.19 to 15.32. Only SW-T1 was ever bracketed, and that bracket found the shared value 20% off and worth 39% of that specimen's error.

The deficiency is specific to the tensile pair, and the arithmetic says why. With σ₀ = KniVm = 11.22 MPa, SW-T1 and SW-T2 run at 5.8× their scale stress while SW-S3 and SW-S4 run at 1.7–2.8×. The shear specimens sit on a usable part of the closure curve. The tensile ones are pinned against the asymptote.

SW-T2 has SW-T1's exact defect, at SW-T1's exact operating stress, with SW-T1's exact constants, and has never been bracketed. Because the constants are identical, 100_01's already-validated Vm and re-solved offset transfer verbatim — a two-line edit.

One related item for the record, since it will draw a referee's eye: axial_bc_penalty is 4.123e11, 5.121e11, 1.0e13 and 1.2e12 across the four specimens. That is a 24× spread in the stiffness of a single loading frame, used as a per-specimen fitting parameter.

How to move forward

Ordered by expected gain per run

  1. Adopt the best runs already on disk

    zero compute

    Three of the four published finals are beaten by probes that are already scored and already comparable. 100_01 differs from 93_01 by exactly two constitutive lines and the output-only flux wiring — it is a legitimate calibration of the same model, not a variant. Campaign mean 4.394 → 3.828, a 12.9% reduction, for the cost of re-running the selection. If the 93-series has to stay frozen as the published set, that needs saying explicitly, because right now the better runs are simply unlabelled.

  2. SW-T1 at Vm=55µm with aperture_scale 0.0155

    1 run

    The two axes are demonstrably orthogonal. Going from 99_01 to 100_02 adds aperture_scale at fixed Vm and moves only Q, 6.15→4.03, leaving all four other channels identical to three digits. That combination was never tried at the winning Vm. Expected mean around 2.4 against 2.69, and the orthogonality makes it close to a sure thing.

  3. Port the Vm bracket to SW-T2

    3 runs

    The highest-confidence unrun experiment in the campaign: same law, same constants, same operating stress, same defect, never tested. Bracket 55 / 62 / 70 µm with the offset re-solved at the 31 MPa seat — the 55 µm numbers transfer verbatim from 100_01. Do not exceed 80 µm; that is where SW-T1 stopped firing, and SW-T2 sits on the same knife edge. Targets the unloading branch, which is 41% of SW-T2's Q error, and dn.

  4. Refit the hydraulic closure law, SW-T1 and SW-T2

    2 runs

    Two constants each, derived from Table 2 rather than swept: Vh 48.8µm / Kh 2.70e11 for SW-T1, 70.4µm / 2.88e11 for SW-T2, with use_nonlinear_normal_closure = true on SW-T1. Cuts the pre-event aperture error 4.4× on SW-T2. Run it after the Vm bracket, not before — both laws move the same aperture and confounding them wastes the arm.

  5. SW-S4: lower peak strength, hold Dc

    2–3 runs

    Onset needs to land at stage 4 with the weakening still spread over stages 4–6. That means peak strength down roughly 1–1.5 MPa at Dc = 74.5 µm unchanged, through JRC or cohesion — not through the weakening path, which 105_04/05/06 have now shown three ways is already correct. This is the only specimen where the published final is genuinely the best available, so it is also the only one where new runs are the sole route.

  6. Report the σ′n–τ redundancy

    zero compute

    State that the two stress channels are one measurement on both sides, give the four-channel mean alongside the five-channel one, and use the θ recovery as an independent validation of the deck geometry against the paper. Better to land this yourselves than to have it raised in review.

What not to run

  • No more SW-S4 weakening arms. Dc down, floor down, and both together all regressed, and Table 2's own τ–ds curve puts the parent's Dc inside the implied 55–98 µm band. That axis is closed.
  • No more Mohr–Coulomb archive-envelope arms. All four of 105_07 to 105_10 regressed against the 94-series transfer baseline. The recovered 67_11 and 83_11 envelopes do not transfer, with or without rate-and-state. The MC comparison stands on the 94-series.
  • No Vm above 80 µm on either tensile specimen. Past that the slip event stops firing and the run scores around 59 — not a fit to interpret, a different experiment.
  • Nothing on this list locally. 16 physical cores, 30 GB, and this campaign has already taken one OOM kill.

Two calls I got wrong

  • I read SW-S4's τ residual as a floor that was set too high. The corrected scoring shows the parent's unloading τ error falling from +0.89 to +0.05 — the floor was already right, and 105_05 pushed it through zero to −1.12. The residual is onset, and it is one stage.
  • I withdrew the axial_bc_penalty bracket on the grounds that SW-S4's τ was not frame-limited. The frame does matter — every specimen carries a non-zero τ error at stage 1 with zero slip anywhere, which can only be elastic — but it is not the lever that pays next, and the 24× spread across four specimens is a provenance problem to disclose rather than a knob to turn.
